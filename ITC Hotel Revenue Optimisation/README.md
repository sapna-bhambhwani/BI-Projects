ITC Hotel Revenue Optimization Analytics – Power BI Dashboard

**Project Overview**


This Power BI project delivers a comprehensive analytics solution for a multi-property hotel chain (ITC Hotels). It provides end-to-end visibility into key operational and financial metrics, covering Occupancy, Revenue, Room Category Performance, Booking Trends, and Cancellations & Lost Revenue.
The dashboard is designed to support data-driven decisions for:


•	Revenue managers


•	Hotel leadership


•	Business analysts


•	Operations & sales teams

**Key Dashboards Included**

**Occupancy & Capacity Analysis**


**Metrics Tracked:**


•	Overall Occupancy Rate (43.5%)


•	WoW & MoM growth trends


•	Occupancy by Room Category (Elite, Premium, Presidential, Standard)


•	Occupancy by City & Property


•	RevPAR vs Occupancy correlation


•	Weekday vs Weekend occupancy patterns


•	WoW Occupancy growth waterfall

**Business Use:**


Helps identify underperforming properties, peak periods & market demand shifts.

**Room Category Performance & Booking Insights**


**Highlights:**


•	Average Lead Time: 3.71 days


•	Average Length of Stay (ALOS): 2.37 days


•	Revenue by Room Class


•	Revenue Trend (Running Total)


•	Lead Time Buckets vs Revenue Realization


•	Weekly Revenue & MoM trend


**Business Use:**


Helps understand booking behavior & optimize pricing & inventory by room class.

**Cancellations & Lost Revenue Analysis**


**Key Insights:**

•	Cancellation Rate: ~25%


•	Lost Revenue: ₹ 498M


•	Weekly cancellation trend


•	Day-type impact (Weekday vs Weekend)


•	Property-wise cancellation heatmap


•	Lost revenue by booking date


**Business Use:**


Identifies revenue leakage, helps implement stronger retention and cancellation policies.


**Strategic Insights Summary**


•	Revenue is heavily driven by Elite & Premium rooms


•	Short-lead bookings (0–3 days) dominate — high dependency on last-minute guests


•	Occupancy growing WoW, but MoM decline needs focus


•	High cancellation-led revenue loss (~₹ 498M) is a critical business risk


•	Weekday cancellations are high, indicating corporate volatility



**Strategic Recommendations**


1.	Incentivize early bookings to reduce reliance on last-minute reservations
2.	Strengthen cancellation policies, especially for high-leakage room types
3.	Launch dynamic pricing & targeted marketing for low-occupancy properties
4.	Use forecasting models for:
    o	Demand prediction
    o	Cancellation probability
5.	Promote longer stays through bundled offers or loyalty points
